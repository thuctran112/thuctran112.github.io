# About

Hi, I'm Melody.

I'm a product designer who's spent most of my life figuring things out without a clear map.

My work lives where systems thinking, strategy, and craft overlap, and I enjoy making things feel simple without losing what makes them work. I've spent the past several years testing that instinct on complex enterprise products. The same instinct comes just as much from the patience and structure I first learned through practicing and teaching piano.

Outside of work, I'm usually chasing the same feeling, figuring something out for the first time:

- Traveling
- Snorkeling
- Escape rooms and board games

If you'd like to talk about my work, or any of the projects here, I'd love to hear from you.
